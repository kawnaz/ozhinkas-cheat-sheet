OZHINKA'S CHEAT SHEET - unofficial fan reference for Stardew Valley 1.6
=======================================================================

TO OPEN
1. Right-click the .zip file and choose "Extract All...". Opening it straight
   from inside the zip will NOT work properly.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click index.html.
   It opens in your web browser. Nothing gets installed.

WHAT'S IN THE FOLDER
  index.html     the dashboard (open this one)
  css/           the look and colours
  js/data.js     game info: villagers, crops, fish, bundles, festivals
  js/gifts.js    every villager's loves, likes, dislikes and hates
  js/villager-info.js  villagers' heart events
  js/schedules.js      villagers' full schedules for every season
  js/animals.js  farm animals, machines and artisan goods
  js/prices.js   item sell prices, including quality prices
  js/app.js      how the dashboard works
  images/        the Egg Festival's painted egg icon; you can add other pictures here to use them offline
  fonts/         optional Stardew Valley font file (see the guide inside)

YOUR PROGRESS
Your checkmarks save automatically in your browser.
To share progress, click the floppy disk button (top right), then "Export progress file".
Send that file to the other person, and they click "Load progress file".

INTERNET
Pictures, the tab icons and the pixel font load from the internet.
Offline, everything still works, just with fewer pictures.
